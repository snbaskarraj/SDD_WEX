# Glossary

Fleetline is a synthetic fleet-card portfolio. None of these companies or sites are real.

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| Approved spend | Purchase amount where the authorization status is Approved. Declines are excluded. |
| Fuel spend | Approved spend across every product, including electricity. |
| Price per gallon | Approved fuel dollars divided by gallons. Electricity has zero gallons and is excluded. |
| Fraud losses | Dollars on transactions flagged as fraud, whether or not the fraud type is skimming. |
| Fraud rate | Fraud losses divided by approved spend. |
| SecureFuel | A card control that sharply reduces fraud in this portfolio. |
| Out of network | A merchant that is not in the acceptance network. |
| EV share | Approved spend on cards whose fuel type is EV, divided by approved spend. |
| Delinquency rate | Invoice dollars with days past due greater than 30, divided by billed dollars. |
| Region | Where the fuel was bought (merchant region) for card questions. The fleet’s home region for invoice questions. |
| As-of date | 30 Sep 2026. “This year” and “last quarter” are computed from this date, not from today. |

## Planted stories

**Texas skimming.** In July 2026, Red Mesa Travel Center in Texas has thirty approved diesel sales of $720, all flagged Skimming, all on a standard (not SecureFuel) card owned by Cinder Basin Transport. Ask “Monthly fraud losses in the Southwest”, then “and for Texas”.

**SecureFuel.** Ask “Fraud rate by SecureFuel status”. The standard rate should sit well above SecureFuel.

**EV share.** Technology fleets run half their cards as EV and those charges are large. Ask “Which industry has the highest EV share?”

**Delinquency.** Enterprise invoices are past 30 days in six of eighteen months; mid-market in two; small in none. Ask “Delinquency rate by segment”.

**West diesel.** Diesel at West merchants is about $0.90 a gallon above diesel in the Midwest. Ask “Diesel vs unleaded price per gallon in the West”.
